PRACTICAL INFORMATION

How to work with us

Availability

Working days 8:00–16:30 Central European Time. Contract customers have on-call support according to the agreed support terms.

Meetings

The initial consultation is online and takes about an hour. We arrange an on-site meeting if the project requires it, for example for an operations analysis.

Languages

We communicate in Czech, Slovak, English, German and Polish.

Confidentiality

We are happy to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you share details of your environment. Please do not include patient data or credentials in your first message.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

From first message to proposal

  1. Reply

    We get back to you with follow-up questions and a proposed consultation date.

  2. Consultation

    We go through your systems, the required connections and operational constraints.

  3. Proposal

    We prepare a solution proposal with scope, timeline and split of responsibilities.

BEFORE THE FIRST MEETING

What helps us understand your situation

You do not need a specification. A short description of your starting point is enough, and we will go through the next steps together in an initial consultation.

  • Type and size of the facility: practice, hospital, laboratory, or a software vendor
  • Which system you use today and whether it has its own interface
  • Which national services you need to connect, for example registries, ISIN or e-prescription
  • Whether you already have certificates and access to test environments
  • Preferred operating model and an approximate timeline
