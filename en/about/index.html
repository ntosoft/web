About

Software that actually connects healthcare providers

NTOsoft has long focused on healthcare information systems and above all on the part of projects that tends to be hardest: reliable communication with the outside world. We understand both outpatient and hospital environments and know that a good system is judged by how it holds up in daily operation.

WHAT WE DO

We specialise in integration and data exchange

Healthcare software no longer stands on its own. A practice or hospital needs to look up a patient in the master registries, issue an electronic prescription, submit a report to a national registry, pass a finding to another provider and bill an insurer. Each of these areas has its own interface, its own type of authentication and its own data processing rules.

We deliver the complete solution, not individual pieces. From analysis and design through development of client modules and the integration layer to testing, deployment and operation. This includes certificate management, handling of responses and error states, code system mapping, queues for working during connection outages and an audit record of every operation. The facility gets a working whole rather than a set of interfaces it has to stitch together on its own.

We work with teams that develop their own software as well as organisations that need to connect existing systems. Depending on the situation we deliver a ready-made solution, an integration layer or expert consulting on design, testing and verification with the operators of national services.

AREAS OF EXPERTISE

Where our experience is deepest

Outpatient and practice systems

Practices need a fast, predictable system that does not slow down an examination. We cover medical records, scheduling, electronic prescriptions and requests, insurer reporting and registry notifications. We place special emphasis on administration being generated from what the clinician records anyway, rather than as a second pass over the data.

Hospital information systems

A hospital brings together many departments, roles and connected systems. We deal with how patient data flows between admission, wards, laboratory, imaging, pharmacy and finance, and how mandatory reports are assembled from it. We can also work with operations that must never stop, including phased migrations and parallel running of old and new systems.

Laboratories and imaging

Laboratory and imaging results are among the most frequently shared data. We connect analyzers and laboratory systems, convert results into structured reports and ensure unambiguous matching of request, sample and result. We take care over correct codes, units and reference ranges, because this is where mapping errors show up most.

Registries and mandatory reporting

Reporting to national registries and systems is a legal obligation for providers that burdens staff. We automate the collection of data from medical records, check completeness before submission and evaluate feedback afterwards. Clinicians see what is fine and what needs completing without having to know the technical details of the interface.

Electronic prescription and requests

Electronic prescriptions, vouchers and requests have a strictly defined lifecycle: issuing, sending, cancelling, dispensing. We design client modules to handle every state including unusual ones, such as repeat dispensing or cancellation after partial dispensing, so the clinician is never left helpless when the central service is down.

Migration and legacy system connectivity

Many facilities run software that was built years ago and still works. We offer gateways that translate to modern interfaces, mapping of older data formats to standards and controlled data migration, including checks that nothing is lost or duplicated in transit.

PROCESS

How a project runs

Healthcare is an environment where mistakes are hard to fix after go-live. That is why we put the emphasis on preparation, testing and phased rollout.

  1. Environment analysis

    We map processes, existing systems, data flows and the facility's obligations. The outcome is a list of interfaces, risks and dependencies.

  2. Design and prototype

    We design the architecture, data model and mappings. Risky parts are validated with a prototype against test interfaces.

  3. Iterative implementation

    We develop in short cycles, each with a working demo and automated tests. Specification changes are incorporated continuously.

  4. Testing and verification

    We run service operators' test scenarios, perform load and security tests and prepare documentation for verification.

  5. Phased rollout

    We go live in stages, first at a selected site. A rollback procedure is ready in case of trouble.

  6. Operations and support

    We monitor communication, watch certificate validity and specification changes and handle incidents within agreed response times.
