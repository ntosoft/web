SOLUTIONS

Solutions for healthcare providers of every size

Three product lines built on a common foundation: a hospital system, a practice system and an integration platform. Each works on its own and all share the same connectivity to national services.

Hospital information system

A system for inpatient facilities that connects admission, wards, ancillary services and finance. Mandatory reports, discharge reports and statements are generated from data recorded during care and sent to national systems via the same integration layer used by practices.

  • Electronic health records
  • Admission, transfer and discharge
  • Laboratory and imaging workflows
  • Billing and reporting
  • Discharge report in HL7 FHIR
  • Registry reports generated from documentation

Outpatient and practice system

A system for GPs, paediatricians and outpatient specialists. Prescriptions, vaccinations, requests and reports are issued straight from the patient record and the status of every submission is visible in one place, without logging in to other portals.

  • Patient record with fast search
  • Electronic prescriptions and referrals
  • Appointments and reminders
  • Clear billing overview
  • Vaccination records in the ISIN vaccination module
  • Overview of submitted reports and their status

Integration platform & API

A standalone layer for software vendors and facilities with their own system. It encapsulates communication with master registries, ISIN, e-prescription, base registries and shared records and exposes it through one documented interface.

  • A unified REST API described in OpenAPI 3
  • Adapters for REST, SOAP/WSDL and HL7 FHIR R4
  • Client certificate and validity management
  • Message queues for working during outages
  • Code system mapping and pre-send validation
  • Audit record and monitoring of every call

ARCHITECTURE

Built on modern foundations

Modular services

Every area, from patient records to e-prescription, runs as a separate service with its own interface. The facility deploys only what it needs and individual parts can be updated without taking the whole system down.

Containers and orchestration

Repeatable deployment from containers, separate test and production environments and updates that can be rolled back if needed. The same procedure applies on own servers and in the cloud.

Cloud or your own servers

Operation on the facility's infrastructure, in the cloud or hybrid, where patient data stays with the facility and only part of the operation moves to the cloud. The choice depends on the facility's requirements and security policy.
