IN PRACTICE

One patient visit, five connections

The physician works in the patient record as usual. The integration layer handles communication with national services in the background and the outcome of each step is visible right in the system.

  1. Admission

    Patient matched in the master registry and identifier stored in the record.

  2. Examination

    Documentation entries from which reports and summaries are later assembled.

  3. Vaccination

    The dose with batch and vaccine code is recorded in the vaccination module.

  4. Prescription

    The e-prescription goes to the central repository and the patient receives an identifier.

  5. Reporting and billing

    Mandatory reports and insurer billing items are generated from data already recorded.

FAQ

What healthcare providers ask us

Do we have to replace our current system to connect?

No. The integration layer can be attached to an existing practice or hospital system via its interface, database or HL7 messages. We only replace a system where the facility itself wants to.

Who arranges certificates and access to national services?

Certificates and permissions are always issued to the healthcare facility by the service operators and relevant certification authorities. We help prepare the documents, install them in a secure store and monitor their validity.

What happens when a national service is unavailable?

The physician keeps working. Requests are queued and sent once the connection is restored, in the correct order and with duplicate checks. For patients who cannot be matched immediately a temporary identifier is used.

Where does the software run and where is data stored?

As the facility chooses: on its own servers, in the cloud or a combination. Sensitive data and certificates can stay in the facility's infrastructure; only communication with national services leaves it.

How quickly can you react to a specification change?

We follow changes to national interfaces continuously and incorporate them into the integration layer. Because all connections are in one place, a single change does not require modifying every application separately.

Can we try the connection first?

Yes. Development and debugging run against operators' test environments and simulators, so the facility sees the result before anything is sent to production.
